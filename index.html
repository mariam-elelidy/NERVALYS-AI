Architecture

From Raw Biology to Clinical Intelligence

Four engineered stages convert heterogeneous multimodal patient data into a structured, interpretable disease representation.

01
Ingest

Inflammatory biomarkers, oxidative stress markers, EMG signals, and clinical metadata are normalized and validated.

TNF-αMDA MUNEMUIMUAPI
02
Encode

Biological signals pass through a denoising Autoencoder. EMG through 1D-CNN + BiLSTM. Each modality encoded separately to preserve causal structure.

Autoencoder1D-CNNBiLSTM
03
Fuse → PV

Cross-modal Transformer attention fuses latents into the Personalized Vector — a structured disease representation unique to each patient.

TransformercHVAEPV
04
Interpret

Multi-head outputs deliver stratification axes, prognosis, risk windows, nerve age, and GNN-based mechanism-to-muscle interaction maps.

SHAPGNNMLP
